Standardization of dotfile distribution and encryption.

# What is the purpose of this?
The project aims to standardize installation and sharing of config files. It is meant to be __modular__ to be *cross platform* and *cross architecture*, cloud agnostic, offline and online. Your configs, your rules. You are the soul owner of this. 

Privacy is a fundamental risk, threat actor and corporations scanning every piece of code on the internet right now, don't give the Internet an idea of what your attack surface might be.

# Who is this for?
Everyone. The project aims for creating an easy and straight forward way to share your configurations and files, no matter how complex the installation process might be.
